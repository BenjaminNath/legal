Delete Your Styck Account

If you want to permanently delete your account and all associated personal data from Styck, you can request data deletion at any time. You do not need to have the app currently installed to complete this request.

How to Request Deletion

Please send an email from the same email address you used to register your app account so we can verify ownership:

Email: styckgolf@gmail.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request

Once received and validated, your account, authentication records, and app data will be fully purged from our systems within 30 days.

What Data Will Be Permanently Deleted

  • Account Profile: Your primary registration email, password hashes, and profile metadata.
  • App Activity: All history, custom settings, user configurations, and preferences linked to your unique user ID.
  • Diagnostic Data: Any logged crash data or performance diagnostics tied to your specific user profile.

Note: Financial transaction history and invoices (if applicable) will be securely retained for administrative and tax compliance purposes as required by local laws.
